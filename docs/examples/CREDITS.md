# Example media

The photos `<tool>.jpg` and the clips `clip-*.mp4` are the inputs of the README screenshots (`app/tests/shots.js`).

The photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
They are cropped and resized (`downloader.jpg` is cropped to leave out a sign). `grade.jpg` is not from Unsplash: it is a
widely shared still of The Weeknd on stage (After Hours til Dawn Tour), photographer and licence unknown, chosen by the
project owner. `grade-look.json` is the look the Grade screenshot applies, made in Grade by the project owner.
