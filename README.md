Halation brings the small creative jobs that usually spill into separate apps into one consistent workspace. Open media, work with it, keep related files close, and export without rebuilding the workflow every time.

## Built for the work between the big apps

Halation is not trying to replace a full editor or DAW. It focuses on the jobs around them: preparing media, cleaning it up, extracting what is useful, converting it, testing presentation, and moving quickly between formats.

- **Image finishing** — grade images, upscale media, remove backgrounds and refine cutouts.
- **Video utilities** — grab frames, interpolate motion and prepare media for other workflows.
- **Audio tools** — separate vocals/instrumentals or four stems, then audition and export them.
- **Conversion** — convert images, audio, video, documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDF pages into useful target formats.
- **Collection & reuse** — keep working files in Library, save Sessions and recover interrupted work.
- **Creative context** — use Cover Test, profiles and visual references without turning the workspace into a browser full of tabs.

Halation also includes **Interpolation**, **Visualizer**, **Cover Test** and related utilities, with a shared interface and export behavior across the workspace.

## Local by design

The images, video and audio opened in Halation are processed locally on the PC. The app does not require an account, login or cloud-processing service for its editing, AI enhancement and export tools, and Halation does not include its own analytics or advertising telemetry.

Some features intentionally use the network when invoked. Downloader and URL analysis contact the relevant services; profile discovery and Cover Test can query public services; update checks read Halation's public GitHub release metadata; and optional engines or models may be downloaded from their upstream hosts.

Read the complete policy in **[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)**.

## Sessions that survive interruption

Halation supports manual Sessions with **Save**, **Save As** and **Open Session**. Saved Sessions can be written automatically while changes are pending, and crash recovery can restore interrupted work on the next launch.

The interface is available in **English and Spanish**.
